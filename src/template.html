Drop your export anywhere on this page

Take Location History (Timeline) out of Google Takeout, or export it from the Timeline screen in Google Maps on your phone, and drop the JSON here. A 90 MB file is fine — it is read in slices and never leaves your machine.

  • Timeline.json — the Android on-device export
  • location-history.json — the iOS on-device export
